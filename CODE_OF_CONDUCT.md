# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We're building something useful together. Everyone who contributes — regardless of experience level, background, or tools they use — deserves a respectful, constructive environment.

---

## Our Standards

**Do this:**
- Give feedback on the work, not the person
- Be direct and specific — vague criticism helps no one
- Welcome newcomers and first-time contributors
- Acknowledge when you're wrong or outdated
- Share context when you disagree

**Don't do this:**
- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissive language
- Gatekeeping — "that's not a real skill" type comments
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project
- Sharing others' private information without permission

---

## Enforcement

Issues that violate this standard will be handled like this:

| Severity | Example | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Dismissive comment | Private reminder |
| Moderate | Repeated disrespect | Warning + temporary restriction |
| Severe | Harassment, threats | Permanent ban |

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, open a private issue or contact the maintainer directly via GitHub.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.

---

## Scope

This applies to all spaces in this repo — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any related community spaces.
