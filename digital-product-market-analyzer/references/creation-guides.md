## SECTION A — CALENDARS, PLANNERS & JOURNALS

### What's Selling
- **Niched planners** outperform generic ones: ADHD planner, homeschool planner, Ramadan planner, fitness planner, budget planner, mom planner, teacher planner
- **Undated planners** sell year-round (no seasonal slump)
- **Digital + printable combo** commands premium pricing

### Tools Needed
| Tool       | Cost    | Best For                         |
|------------|---------|----------------------------------|
| Canva Pro  | ~$13/mo | Beginners, fast production       |
| Adobe InDesign | $55/mo | Professional-grade layouts   |
| Google Slides | Free | Simple, easy to share         |
| Affinity Publisher | $70 one-time | Budget pro option     |

### Step-by-Step: Create a Printable Planner

**PHASE 1 — Research (1–2 days)**
1. Search Etsy for "[niche] planner" — sort by "Best Sellers"
2. Note: page count, price range, what pages are included
3. Read reviews on competitors — what do buyers love/request?
4. Choose your niche and unique angle (e.g., "ADHD weekly planner with brain dump section")

**PHASE 2 — Plan Your Pages (half a day)**
Common planner pages to include:
- Cover page
- Introduction / How to Use
- Year at a Glance (annual calendar)
- Monthly Overview (12 months)
- Weekly Spreads
- Daily Schedule pages
- Habit Tracker
- Goal Setting pages
- Notes / Lined pages
- Budget Tracker
- Gratitude / Reflection prompts

**PHASE 3 — Design in Canva (2–5 days)**
1. Open Canva → Create Design → Custom Size
   - A4 (210 x 297mm) or US Letter (8.5 x 11in)
   - For spiral-bound look: add a 0.5in gutter on binding side
2. Choose a cohesive color palette (3–5 colors max)
3. Select 2–3 fonts: one display (headings) + one readable (body)
4. Design one page template → duplicate and modify for each section
5. Use Canva's "Elements" for decorative touches (florals, geometric, etc.)
6. Keep it clean — less is more for usability

**PHASE 4 — Export & Prepare (1 day)**
1. Export as PDF (Print quality, CMYK if possible)
2. Create a second version: Digital (RGB, with hyperlinks if applicable)
3. Compress the PDF using tools like SmallPDF or ILovePDF
4. Create a mockup (Canva has mockup frames, or use Placeit.net)

**PHASE 5 — List on Etsy**
1. Title: Include keywords first — e.g., "ADHD Planner Printable Undated Weekly Planner PDF Digital Download"
2. Use all 13 tags (long-tail keywords)
3. Price: $8–$18 for a full planner bundle
4. Upload mockup photos (minimum 5, lifestyle + flat lay style)
5. Write description: Benefits first, then features, then technical specs
6. Add FAQ section (common questions: "Can I print at home?", "What size is this?")

### Step-by-Step: Create a Digital Calendar

**Types that sell:**
- Perpetual wall calendar (no year = sells forever)
- Monthly calendar pages (set of 12)

**Process (2–3 days total):**

2. In Canva: Use the Calendar template as a base
3. Design 12 monthly pages with consistent branding
4. Add holidays relevant to your audience (search "2025 2026 holidays")

6. Bundle with matching desktop wallpapers for premium value
7. Price: $4–$12 for printable set; $8–$20 for digital interactive

## SECTION B — E-BOOKS, GUIDES & WORKBOOKS

### What's Selling
- "How to" guides in profitable niches (make money, productivity, health, relationships)
- Niche recipe e-books (keto, vegan, air fryer)
- "Starter guides" for new hobbies/skills
- Workbooks that accompany courses or YouTube channels
- Lead magnets (free e-books that build email lists)

### Tools Needed
- **Canva** (easiest — has e-book templates)
- **Google Docs → Export PDF** (free, simple)
- **Microsoft Word → PDF** (accessible)
- **Adobe InDesign** (professional typography)
- **Atticus or Vellum** (specifically for book formatting for KDP)

### Step-by-Step: Create an E-book

**PHASE 1 — Topic Research (1 day)**
1. Search Amazon → Kindle store → your niche → sort by Best Sellers
2. Read free previews and reviews (what's missing? what do readers want MORE of?)
3. Search Etsy: "[niche] guide PDF" to see digital guide demand
4. Use Google Trends to validate topic interest
5. Choose a topic where you have genuine knowledge OR can research thoroughly

**PHASE 2 — Outline & Write (3–10 days depending on length)**
Recommended structure:
```
- Cover Page
- Table of Contents (with hyperlinks)
- Introduction: Promise what reader will learn
- Chapter 1–7 (aim for 5–10 chapters)
  - Subheadings every 300–400 words
  - Bullet points, callout boxes, numbered lists
  - Actionable tips in every chapter
- Conclusion + Next Steps
- About the Author
- Resources / References
- Bonus: Checklist or Quick-Reference Sheet
```
Aim for: 5,000–15,000 words for a solid e-book (30–80 pages with design)

**PHASE 3 — Design (1–3 days)**
1. Open Canva → search "e-book" templates
2. Choose a template matching your niche aesthetic
3. Paste your text, adjust formatting
4. Add: chapter divider pages, pull quotes, icons, infographics
5. Maintain consistent typography throughout
6. Design a professional cover (this is the #1 sales driver)

**PHASE 4 — Export & Sell**
- Export as PDF (Canva → Download → PDF Print)
- For Amazon KDP: Export as .epub using Calibre (free tool) or Atticus
- Platform: Gumroad, Payhip, Etsy, or Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing
- Price: $7–$47 depending on length and niche value

## SECTION C — CANVA TEMPLATES & DESIGN ASSETS

### What's Selling
- **Instagram/TikTok templates** — Reels covers, story sets, post bundles
- **Pitch deck / presentation templates** — Business & startup focused
- **Resume & CV templates** — Always in demand
- **Brand identity kits** — Logo + color palette + font combos
- **Media kit templates** — For influencers
- **Wedding stationery** — Invitations, RSVP, signage

### Step-by-Step: Create a Canva Template Bundle

**PHASE 1 — Niche Research**
1. Go to Creative Market and Etsy → search "Canva templates" → Best Sellers
2. Pick a niche: social media templates for [industry] (e.g., "Canva templates for real estate agents")
3. Niched templates outsell generic ones 5:1

**PHASE 2 — Design the Bundle (3–7 days)**
1. Open Canva → create your designs
2. Aim for 10–30 templates per bundle for value perception
3. Keep a consistent style: same font family, same color palette
4. Design at the correct platform dimensions:
   - Instagram Post: 1080 x 1080px
   - Instagram Story: 1080 x 1920px
   - Pinterest: 1000 x 1500px
   - LinkedIn Post: 1200 x 628px

**PHASE 3 — Deliver the Template**
1. In Canva: Click "Share" → "Template link" → this gives buyers an editable copy
2. Package the link in a PDF "Instructions" document
3. Never share as a downloaded file — share the template LINK

**PHASE 4 — List & Sell**
- Etsy: List as "Digital Download" (deliver PDF with Canva link)
- Gumroad: Sell the PDF instruction document with embedded link
- Creative Market: Submit for review (higher quality bar, better commission)
- Price: $9–$47 per bundle; premium niche-specific kits: $47–$97

## SECTION D — NOTION & SPREADSHEET TEMPLATES

### What's Selling
- **Notion Life OS** — All-in-one personal productivity dashboards
- **Notion CRM** — Small business client management
- **Budget Spreadsheet** — Monthly/annual financial trackers
- **Content Calendar** — For creators and marketers
- **Project Management Template** — Freelancers and agencies
- **Habit Tracker** — Daily/weekly accountability systems

### Step-by-Step: Create a Notion Template

**PHASE 1 — Design in Notion**
1. Build your template inside your own free Notion account
2. Focus on: linked databases, filtered views, formulas, rollups
3. Add: a "Start Here" page with instructions
4. Include: pre-filled example data so buyers see it populated
5. Use Notion icons and covers for visual appeal

**PHASE 2 — Duplicate for Delivery**
1. Click "Share" on the Notion page → enable "Allow Duplicate as Template"
2. Copy the shareable link — this is what customers receive
3. Package this link inside a clean PDF with setup instructions

**PHASE 3 — Price & Sell**
- Simple single template: $7–$19
- Multi-page system: $27–$67
- Premium Life OS / Business OS: $47–$197
- Best platforms: Gumroad, Etsy, your own website

### Step-by-Step: Create a Budget Spreadsheet (Google Sheets / Excel)

1. Build in Google Sheets (shareable, no software required for buyers)
2. Include: income tracker, expense categories, monthly summary, charts
3. Use data validation dropdowns, conditional formatting (auto-colors)
4. Protect formula cells so buyers don't accidentally break it
5. Deliver as: Google Sheets template link OR Excel .xlsx file
6. Price: $7–$27; premium annual finance system: $37–$67

## SECTION E — AMAZON KDP (LOW-CONTENT BOOKS)

### What's Selling on KDP
- Notebooks (lined, dotted, blank)
- Journals with prompts
- Puzzle books (word searches, crosswords, sudoku)
- Composition books (niche covers)
- Log books (password log, vehicle mileage, workout log)
- Coloring books (adult and children's)
- Activity books for kids

### Step-by-Step: Publish on Amazon KDP

**PHASE 1 — Research with Tools**
- Use **Publisher Rocket** ($97 one-time) or **Book Bolt** ($9.99/mo)
- Find low-competition, high-demand niches (BSR < 100,000)
- Look for: profitable keywords with weak existing competition

**PHASE 2 — Create Interior (the pages)**
1. Use **Book Bolt's Studio** (drag-and-drop interior builder)
   OR use **Canva** with KDP-specific dimensions:
   - 6 x 9in (most popular) or 8.5 x 11in
2. Create 100–120 pages minimum for paperback
3. For lined notebooks: simple ruled lines, maybe a header per page
4. Export as PDF (must meet KDP's bleed/trim specs)

**PHASE 3 — Create Cover**
1. Download KDP's cover template (based on page count + trim size)
2. Design in Canva or Photoshop using the template dimensions
3. Must include: front cover, spine (width depends on pages), back cover
4. Include a barcode placeholder (KDP adds automatically)

**PHASE 4 — Upload to KDP**
1. Go to kdp.amazon.com → Add New Title → Paperback
2. Fill in: title, subtitle, author name (can be pen name), description
3. Upload interior PDF and cover PDF
4. Set pricing:
   - $6.99–$9.99 for standard notebooks (70% royalty on ebooks, 60% print)
   - Higher for activity/puzzle books ($12.99–$19.99)
5. Go wide: enable distribution to Amazon worldwide markets

**Volume strategy:** Successful KDP sellers publish 50–200+ books. Each is passive once live.

| Udemy       | Free        | 37–50% cut      | Marketplace traffic   |

### Step-by-Step: Create a Mini-Course

**PHASE 1 — Validate Before You Build**
1. Post on social: "Would you pay $X to learn [topic] in [timeframe]?"
2. Pre-sell with early bird pricing (even 5–10 pre-sales = validation)
3. Build only after validation

**PHASE 2 — Structure Your Course**
- 5–10 video lessons (10–20 minutes each) for a mini-course
- Each lesson = 1 actionable concept
- Include: worksheets, templates, or checklists as bonuses
- Record with Loom (free) or Descript for talking-head + screen capture

**PHASE 3 — Record & Edit**
- Equipment: decent microphone ($50+ USB mic makes huge difference), ring light, clean background
- Edit with: Descript (transcript-based editing), CapCut (free), or DaVinci Resolve (free pro)
- Keep lessons tight — no filler

**PHASE 4 — Host & Sell**
1. Upload to Teachable / Kajabi / Thinkific
2. Set up a sales page with: pain point → solution → social proof → offer
3. Price: $27–$97 for mini-course; $197–$497 for full course
4. Create a free lead magnet to build email list → sell course to list

## REALISTIC INCOME PROJECTIONS

### Etsy Printables (starting from zero)
- Month 1–3: $0–$50/mo (building listings, no reviews)
- Month 4–6: $50–$300/mo (growing reviews, more listings)
- Month 7–12: $300–$1,500/mo (20+ listings, good reviews, SEO kicking in)
- Year 2+: $1,500–$5,000+/mo (consistent + scaling)

### Amazon KDP (Low-Content Books)
- 1–10 books: $10–$50/mo
- 50+ books: $200–$800/mo
- 200+ books: $1,000–$4,000/mo
- Key: volume + research = success

### Courses (with existing audience)
- Mini-course to email list of 500: $500–$2,500 per launch
- Full course to list of 2,000: $5,000–$20,000 per launch
- Evergreen (always open) funnel: $1,000–$10,000/mo passively

**Reality check:** Most successful digital product sellers treat it like a business — consistent effort for 6–12 months before meaningful income. Treat month 1–3 as investment, not payout.
